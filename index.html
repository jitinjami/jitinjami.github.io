Jitin Jami

Doctoral Researcher at Machine Learning and Data Analytics Lab at FAU, Erlangen.
Research Assistant at Chair of Ambient Assisted Living & Medical Assistance Systems at University of Bayreuth.

I’m Jitin, a Doctoral Researcher at FAU’s Machine Learning and Data Analytics Lab in Erlangen since October 2023. Since November 2025, I am a Research Assistant at the Chair of Ambient Assisted Living & Medical Assistance Systems at the University of Bayreuth.
headed by Prof. Dr. Heike Leutheuser .
I’m actively involved in the SWODDYS project , a collaboration with Uniklinikum Regenburg and PreSens GmbH. The project is funded by Bayerische Forschungsstiftung .
Contact: jitin [dot] jami [at] fau.de; jitin [dot] jami [at] uni-bayreuth.de
