I'm Jitin, a Doctoral Researcher at the <a href="https://www.mad.tf.fau.de">Machine Learning and Data Analytics Lab</a> at FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg since October 2023. Since November 2025, I am also a Research Assistant at the <a href="https://www.ai11.uni-bayreuth.de/en/index.html">Chair of Machine Learning in Medicine</a> at the University of Bayreuth, headed by <a href="http://leutheuser.eu">Prof. Dr. Heike Leutheuser</a>.

My research focuses on AI-assisted chronic wound assessment and on making machine learning evaluation more reliable, in particular how the way we split data affects benchmark results in image segmentation and graph learning.

I'm actively involved in the <a href="https://www.mad.tf.fau.de/research/projects/swoddys-smart-wound-dressing-incorporating-dye-based-sensors/">SWODDYS project</a>, a collaboration with <a href="https://www.ukr.de/en/">Uniklinikum Regensburg</a> and <a href="https://www.presens.de">PreSens GmbH</a>, funded by the <a href="https://www.forschungsstiftung.bayern.de">Bayerische Forschungsstiftung</a>.
